BANKING ACCOUNT FAQ

Savings Account

A savings account is a deposit account used for keeping money safely while earning applicable interest.

Account Balance

Customers can check their current account balance through the banking application, internet banking, ATM, or by contacting customer support.

Account Statement

Customers can view or download their account transaction statement through the banking application or internet banking.

Account Closure

Customers who want to close their account should contact the bank through an authorized branch or customer service channel. Any applicable outstanding charges or obligations must be settled before account closure.

KYC

Customers must maintain valid KYC information according to applicable banking requirements. Customers may be asked to provide valid identity and address documentation when updating their KYC information.
