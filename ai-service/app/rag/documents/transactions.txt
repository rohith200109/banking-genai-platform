BANKING TRANSACTIONS

Deposits

A deposit adds money to the customer's bank account. The account balance is updated after a successful transaction.

Withdrawals

A withdrawal removes money from the customer's bank account. The transaction is completed only when the bank successfully processes the request.

Transaction Status

A transaction may have statuses such as COMPLETED, PENDING, or FAILED.

Completed Transaction

A COMPLETED transaction has been successfully processed by the banking system.

Pending Transaction

A PENDING transaction is still being processed. Customers should wait for the transaction to be completed or contact the bank if the transaction remains pending for an extended period.

Failed Transaction

A FAILED transaction was not successfully processed. Customers should check the transaction details and contact the bank if required.

Transaction History

Customers can review their transaction history through the banking application or internet banking.
