PERSONAL LOAN INFORMATION

Personal Loan

A personal loan is an unsecured loan that can be used for eligible personal financial requirements.

Eligibility

Personal loan eligibility depends on factors such as income, employment information, credit history, existing financial obligations, and the bank's applicable lending policy.

Required Documents

Applicants may be required to provide identity proof, address proof, income proof, employment-related documents, and recent bank statements.

Interest Rate

The applicable personal loan interest rate depends on the customer's eligibility, credit profile, loan amount, tenure, and the bank's prevailing lending policy.

Loan Tenure

The available loan tenure depends on the bank's lending policy and the customer's eligibility.

Loan Approval

Submission of an application does not guarantee loan approval. The bank evaluates the application and supporting information before making a lending decision.

Prepayment

Customers should contact the bank to understand applicable prepayment terms, charges, and conditions before making an early repayment.
